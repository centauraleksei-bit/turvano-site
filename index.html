Construction draw verification software for private lenders.

Software that lenders use to review renovation evidence against the scope of work. Contractors capture photo evidence in a mobile app; a reviewer records a finding for each line item of the declared scope; the lender receives a review report. Funding decisions stay with the lender.

hello@turvano.com

How it works

Who it is for. TURVANO is offered to lenders and financial institutions only. It is not offered to the general public, including buyers or sellers of real estate.

Evidence. Contractors capture BEFORE / DURING / AFTER photo evidence of renovation work items, tied to the declared scope of work. The mobile app works offline on site and sends records when connectivity returns.

Integrity. Every evidence record is confirmed by the server with a SHA-256 fingerprint; evidence is tamper-evident within TURVANO’s trust boundary. Photos are stripped of EXIF metadata before upload; location is attached only when the person switches it on for that photo.

Review. A reviewer checks the captured evidence against the declared scope of work and records a finding for each line item. The review report is delivered as a PDF with per-photo fingerprints and the limits of the report stated on the document.

Status. Pre-launch. The Android app and web cabinet are in device testing ahead of the first US pilots.
